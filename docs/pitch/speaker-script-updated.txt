Here's what we've built. Choose your region, billing situation, activities, and availability. WattHabit finds a lower-price window that fits your routine.

Slide 4: Real prices. Advice that fits your day.
1:55–2:25 / Technical presenter

We built WattHabit with Next.js and TypeScript. It checks real Swedish prices in fifteen-minute intervals and finds a complete window for your activity.

Qwen, through Groq, helps choose a short, reviewed explanation. If AI is unavailable, written guidance keeps the app working.

We also analyzed four weeks of synthetic data from eight flats and twenty-five fictional students. That helped us focus on background use, lighting, and flexible timing.

Demo cue: The AI doesn't invent prices or savings. Server calculations verify price facts. The synthetic dataset informs scenario design and is separate from the live price feed.

Slide 5: Three habits. About 11% in one modeled scenario.
2:25–3:10 / Ignes

What could the habits save? In this annual illustration, we switch off a separate standby device, put an unused computer setup to sleep, and turn off unused lights.

With the assumptions shown here, that's 108 kilowatt-hours per student per year. Against an illustrative 1,000-kilowatt-hour baseline, it's almost eleven percent.

At two kronor per kilowatt-hour, that's 216 kronor.

These are modeled opportunities. Moving laundry may save extra money on a matching tariff, but it doesn't reduce electricity use by itself.

Demo cue: Keep the assumptions and illustrative baseline visible. The app's daily examples use longer durations than this annual illustration. Carbon can stay on the slide; don't spend time reading every number. If challenged, open 'View assumptions & sources'.

Slide 6: Good habits stay free. Plus adds local perks.
3:10–3:40 / Business presenter

The energy guidance stays free. That includes price windows, reminders, coaching, challenges, digital achievements, and Savings Radar.

We're planning a Plus tier at around five dollars a month, with future partner perks like coffee offers and student experiences.

So there's a reason to take part even when electricity is in your rent. We'll need local partners and clear offer limits to make those perks work.
