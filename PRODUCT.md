# WattHabit product context

## Register

product

## Users

Swedish university students, including renters with electricity included in rent. The app should be understandable on a phone in twenty seconds, with no account required for the demo.

## Product Purpose

Help students choose useful energy habits, understand sourced Swedish spot-price windows, ask a grounded coach questions, and explore participation-based demo rewards. PROJECT.md and docs/CONTRACT.md remain the product and integration sources of truth.

## Brand Personality

Calm, warm, adult-friendly. A clean lifestyle-rewards experience with coffeehouse accents. The animated cup represents reward progress; it is not the brand identity or the only future reward.

## Anti-references

Avoid childish virtual pets, giant analytics dashboards, invented partner promotions, crowded component grids, and unlabelled simulated savings or vouchers.

## Design Principles

- Make today's action and the coach easy to find.
- Use the cup to explain participation progress.
- Keep live, cached, unavailable and demo states explicit.
- Preserve the distinction between shifting energy, saving energy and earning stamps.
- Keep core interactions working without service credentials.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile-first at 375, 390 and 430 CSS pixels, with useful desktop layouts. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, screen-reader labels, reduced-motion support, and readable contrast. Do not rely on color alone for price or stamp meaning.
